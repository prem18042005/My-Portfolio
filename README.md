# Personal Portfolio Website

A modern, responsive personal portfolio website showcasing my profile,
skills, projects, experience, and graphic design work. The design
focuses on a classic dark theme with elegant styling, smooth animations,
and a clean user experience.

## ✨ Features

-   **Professional introduction** --- a dedicated section to introduce
    myself.
-   **About me** --- profile information and background.
-   **Skills** --- showcase of technical and creative skills.
-   **Projects** --- highlight selected academic and personal projects.
-   **Experience** --- graphic design experience and other relevant
    work.
-   **Graphic design gallery** --- a dedicated section to display
    creative work.
-   **Responsive layout** --- designed to work across desktop, tablet,
    and mobile screens.
-   **Animations and interactions** --- smooth scrolling and visual
    effects.
-   **Contact section** --- links for visitors to connect.
